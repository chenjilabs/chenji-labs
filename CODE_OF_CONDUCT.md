# Code of Conduct

This project exists to help students learn. Everyone taking part, including teachers, students, contributors and maintainers, is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Not harass, demean or exclude anyone, or share others' private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private channel listed on the repository's profile, or open a private security advisory if no contact is listed.
